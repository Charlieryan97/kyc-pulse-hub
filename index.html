Target Operating Context & Requirements

A precise breakdown of the specific Irish customer segment, their daily manual compliance burden, checkable business requirements, and verified existing market alternatives.

Customer Segment Breakdown

Narrow User Segment: Junior Compliance Officers and KYC Analysts working at boutique Irish financial advisory firms and credit unions (5 to 20 total staff).
Decision Maker / Buyer: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) or Managing Director who holds legal regulatory liability under the CJA (AML/CFT) Acts.

5 Checkable System Requirements

Core capabilities any viable solution must provide:

  • REQ-1 (Centralized Directory): Centralize corporate client files into a single dashboard view with instant search.
  • REQ-2 (Automated Date Engine): Calculate review due dates automatically based on risk level (High = 1Yr, Med = 2Yrs, Low = 3Yrs).
  • REQ-3 (Visual Warning Flags): Auto-generate visual badges for Overdue, Due Soon (< 30 Days), and Compliant status.
  • REQ-4 (Review Sign-Off & Intake): Allow compliance staff to register new entities and record review sign-offs with one click.
  • REQ-5 (State Retention): Retain updated review dates and added profiles across browser sessions using HTML5 LocalStorage.
